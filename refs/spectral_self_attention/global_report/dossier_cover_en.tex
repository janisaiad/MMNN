\textbf{Volume I --- Attention without an MLP.}
Necessary and sufficient condition for all equilibria, finite layer and continuous
time, spectral--Gram reduction in the case
$Q^{\mathsf T}K=V=V^{\mathsf T}$, polygonal forms, precise comparison with Altafini,
and a stable internal cycle produced by attention alone when score and value are untied.

\textbf{Volume II --- Serial attention-then-MLP block.}
Exact equations for all fixed points and cycles, taxonomy of four families, audit down
to layers 4,096 times weaker, continuous cycles, chaos and hyperchaos, multihead maps,
many-token limit, alternative kernels, observables, MLP training, a first Muon step,
and slow OU weights.

separately. The finite census contains 214,528 independently sampled models and
2,844,160 initial trajectories. Recurrent continuous dynamics is assessed by direct
integration, tangent spectra, perturbations, return-time scaling, and basin tests.
