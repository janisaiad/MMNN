\section{Experiments}\label{sec:experiments}

This section presents numerical illustrations. All scripts are headless (matplotlib ``Agg'' backend). Setup: finite-width NTK scripts use a \textbf{3-layer (2 ReLU)} RF-LR architecture with hidden width 12000--20000 so that finite-width variance is small; deterministic proxy scripts iterate the recursion to large \(k\) (e.g.\ \(k_{\max}=4000\) or \(L=200\)). Bottleneck rank \(r\) is swept as indicated; \(n\) is the number of inputs, \(d\) the input dimension. Scripts in the first subsection are \textbf{deterministic}; the rest are \textbf{finite-width Monte Carlo} (they illustrate fluctuation scales and do not constitute proofs).

\subsection{Correlation alignment and equicorrelated spectrum (Theorem~\ref{thm:rflr-depth-scaling-mean}, Corollary~\ref{cor:conditioning-equicorrelated-highdim})}

\textbf{What is computed.} Deterministic ReLU EOC correlation recursion \(\rho_k=\varrho(\rho_{k-1})\) and proxy NTK recursion; diagnostics \(w_k/k\), \(k|\Theta^{(k)}-\Theta_\star(r)|\), \(rk(\Theta_{\mathrm{diag}}^{(k)}-\Theta_{\mathrm{off}}^{(k)})\). For the equicorrelated model, eigenvalues \(\lambda_1=\Theta^{(L)}(1)+(n-1)\Theta^{(L)}(\rho_0)\), \(\lambda_\perp=\Theta^{(L)}(1)-\Theta^{(L)}(\rho_0)\) (e.g.\ \(n=64\), \(\rho_0=0\)).

\subsection{Condition number \(\kappa\) (proxy vs empirical; equicorrelated, high-dim, non-equicorrelated)}

\textbf{What is computed.} Condition number on \(\mathbf{1}^\perp\) vs \(r\). \textbf{Equicorrelated:} \(n=32\), \(d=64\), \(\rho_0=0\); proxy \(\kappa_\perp=1\) (Corollary~\ref{cor:conditioning-equicorrelated-highdim}); empirical mean \(\pm\) std over 40 initializations (Theorem~\ref{thm:equicorrelated-op-bound}). \textbf{High-dim spherical:} i.i.d.\ uniform on \(S^{d-1}\), \(d=256,128\); \(\kappa_\perp\approx 1\). \textbf{Non-equicorrelated:} Clustered design (4 clusters), \(n=48\), \(d=64\), 30 trials; \(\kappa\ge\Omega(r\cdot L)\) (Proposition~\ref{prop:condition-number-depth}).

\subsection{Kernel regression risk and analytic Fisher/Kibble constants}

\textbf{Kernel regression:} Fix a target (linear or low-degree polynomial on the sphere); fit kernel ridge regression with the empirical RF-LR NTK Gram; plot test MSE vs \(r\). 64 train, 256 test, \(d=32\), 20 trials. Validates that as \(r\) grows and the Gram concentrates, risk improves. \textbf{Fisher/Kibble:} Closed-form curves (no sampling): Fisher density \(p(\hat{\rho}\mid \rho,r)\) (\({}_2F_1\)), Kibble joint density (modified Bessel \(I_\nu\)). \textbf{\(I(r)\) and spectral decay:} \(I(r)\) and \(\sqrt{r}\,I(r)\) vs \(r\); \(|2c_1(r)|\) (Puiseux spectral-decay amplitude). Paper: Remark~\ref{rmk:fisher-kibble-law}, Section~\ref{appendix:I-r-scaling}.
