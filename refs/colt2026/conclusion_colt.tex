\section{Conclusion}

We established an NTK theory for RF-LR: (i)~the mean NTK induces the same RKHS as the shallow ReLU kernel via a hypergeometric Puiseux expansion; (ii)~\( I(r) \sim 1/\sqrt{r} \) tightens concentration as \( r \) grows; (iii)~sub-Gaussian concentration in \( r \); (iv)~spike--Marchenko--Pastur spectrum. RF-LR achieves the kernel regime with \( O(rN) \) parameters instead of \( O(N^2) \).
