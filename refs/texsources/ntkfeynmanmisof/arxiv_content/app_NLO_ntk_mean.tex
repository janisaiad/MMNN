\section{NTK mean at next to leading order}
\label{app:NLO_ntk_mean}
As disccused in section \ref{sec-finitewidth-corrections-NTK}, the use of the Feynman rules introduced in the main text, and the selection rules of Appendix \ref{app:feynman_rules} constrain the number of diagrams one can construct for the case of two NTK external lines at leading order in $\frac{1}{n}$, to those displayed in \eqref{firstcorrectionntk}.

Next, we provide the algebraic formulae associated to each diagram in such expansion:
