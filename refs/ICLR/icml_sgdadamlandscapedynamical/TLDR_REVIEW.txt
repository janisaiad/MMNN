TL;DR: REJECT

Main theorem is conditional: "if convergence occurs, then to global minimizer." No conditions for when convergence actually happens. Section 2.5 is essentially empty.

Requires all mixing matrices frozen as random features. This is a severe architectural restriction, not a "solution." Most practical low-rank methods train both factors.

Excludes ReLU (most common activation). Only vague high-probability remark, not quantified.

Proofs incomplete. Many are sketches relying on unverified hypotheses.

Experiments too narrow: only 1D synthetic functions. No validation that convergence occurs. Missing comparisons with full-rank networks.

Quantitative bound has exponential factor e^{K_T(1+rK)} ≈ e^{1000}, making it meaningless.

Theoretical framework is sound but results are too limited. Conditional convergence without sufficient conditions undermines the contribution.
