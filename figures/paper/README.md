# Publication Figures for Low-Rank NTK Paper

This directory contains all publication-quality figures demonstrating the theoretical results of the paper.

## Generated Figures

### Plot 1: Rank-Driven Concentration (Exponential Decay)
**Files**: `fig_plot1_rank_concentration.{pdf,png}`

**Purpose**: Visually proves Theorem 3.2 (exponential concentration in rank)

**Content**:
- Demonstrates concentration rate $\mathbb{P}(|W-1| \geq \epsilon) \leq 4\exp(-r\epsilon^2/8)$
- Multiple curves for different deviation thresholds $\epsilon \in \{0.1, 0.2, 0.5, 1.0\}$
- Contrasts exponential decay with classical $O(1/\sqrt{r})$ rate
- Log-log scale shows dramatic improvement over polynomial concentration

**Key message**: Exponential concentration—not just polynomial improvement

---

### Plot 2: Three-Layer NTK Concentration
**Files**: `fig_plot2_ntk_concentration.{pdf,png}`

**Purpose**: Shows how empirical NTK concentrates around deterministic limit via Fisher-Kibble decoupling

**Content**:
- Three panels showing different ranks $r \in \{16, 32, 64\}$
- Empirical NTK mean with $\pm 2\sigma$ confidence bands
- Deterministic limit $K_\infty(\rho)$ overlay
- Variance shrinks as $O(1/r)$ with increasing rank

**Key message**: Fisher-Kibble decoupling provides rank-driven concentration

---

### Plot 3: Spectral Decay (RKHS Equivalence)
**Files**: `fig_plot3_spectral_decay.{pdf,png}`

**Purpose**: Proves Corollary 3.5—same RKHS despite depth

**Content**:
- Eigenvalue decay on log-log scale
- Multiple curves for depths $L \in \{1, 2, 3, 5, 10\}$
- All curves follow same $k^{-d}$ power law
- Perfect overlap demonstrates RKHS equivalence

**Key message**: Depth provides no RKHS advantage—counterintuitive but theoretically proven

---

### Plot 4: Marchenko-Pastur Spectrum (Spike-Bulk Structure)
**Files**: `fig_plot4_mp_spectrum.{pdf,png}`

**Purpose**: Visualizes Theorem 4.2—deformed MP law with spike

**Content**:
- Histogram of bulk eigenvalues with theoretical MP density overlay
- Prominent spike at $\lambda_{\text{spike}} \approx n \cdot K_\infty(0)$ (vertical line)
- Clear separation between $O(n)$ spike and $O(1)$ bulk
- Configuration: $n=1024$, $r=1024$, $\gamma=n/r$

**Key message**: Spike-bulk structure validates random matrix theory predictions

---

### Plot 5: Computational Efficiency
**Files**: `fig_plot5_efficiency.{pdf,png}`

**Purpose**: Shows parameter budget advantage of RF-LR over MLP

**Content**:
- Log-log plot of parameter count vs width $N$
- RF-LR curves: $O(rN)$ for $r \in \{10, 30, 100\}$
- MLP baseline: $O(N^2)$
- Quadratic gap demonstrates dramatic parameter savings

**Key message**: Entry cost to kernel regime—$O(rN)$ vs $O(N^2)$ quadratic savings

---

### Plot 6: Fisher-Kibble Decoupling
**Files**: `fig_plot6_fisher_kibble.{pdf,png}`

**Purpose**: Shows independence of angular and radial components

**Content**:
- Scatter plot of angular correlation $\hat{\rho}_r$ (Fisher) vs radial product $w_r$ (Kibble)
- Circular scatter pattern (no correlation) proves independence
- Horizontal line at $\mathbb{E}[w_r] = 1$ shows radial concentration
- Configuration: $r=32$

**Key message**: Angular and radial components are independent—fundamental decoupling (Lemma 2.1)

---

### Plot 7: Mean NTK vs Deterministic Limit (Puiseux Expansion)
**Files**: `fig_plot7_puiseux.{pdf,png}`

**Purpose**: Shows how mean NTK differs but preserves RKHS near boundary

**Content**:
- Upper panel: Mean NTK $\tilde{\Theta}^{(2)}$ and deterministic $K_\infty$ vs $\rho \in [0.9, 1.0]$
- Lower panel: Log-log plot of difference showing $t^{1/2}$ scaling ($t = 1-\rho$)
- Both curves exhibit same leading term behavior
- Configuration: $r=32$

**Key message**: Same $t^{1/2}$ leading term → same RKHS despite different higher-order corrections (Corollary 3.4)
